# Tilli Consolidated Privacy Policy

Draft date: May 11, 2026

Status: Draft for internal review and legal review

## 1. Scope and Policy Owner

This Tilli Consolidated Privacy Policy describes how Tilli collects, uses, discloses, stores, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with:

- Tilli websites, landing pages, contact forms, downloads, and online properties;
- customer, prospect, partner, vendor, and applicant interactions;
- Tilli software products and production services, including XDEX, TilliPay, tilliX, TilliBeacon, tilliArch, Nudge, JAREIS-enabled features, and related APIs, portals, and support channels; and
- future Tilli software products, modules, add-ons, and production deployments unless a more specific signed privacy or data-processing document expressly states otherwise.

For United States operations, the relevant entity is **Utilli LLC (doing business as Tilli)**. For India operations, the relevant entity is **Tilli Software India Private Limited**. Those entities are referred to in this policy individually or collectively, as applicable, as **Tilli** or **Tilli Software**.

This policy is intended to be a public-facing umbrella privacy policy. It may be supplemented by:

- a data processing addendum;
- a business associate agreement;
- a customer contract;
- a cookie notice;
- a job applicant notice;
- a product-specific privacy notice; or
- another signed or expressly incorporated privacy addendum.

If a more specific signed or expressly incorporated document conflicts with this policy for a defined processing activity, the more specific document controls for that activity.

## 2. Roles and Context

Tilli may act in different roles depending on the relationship and the product workflow:

- as a controller or business collecting personal information directly from website visitors, prospects, applicants, vendors, and business contacts;
- as a service provider, processor, or similar vendor handling customer-directed data through the Services;
- as an independent compliance actor for fraud, sanctions, AML, or security review where required by law, payment network requirements, processor requirements, or Tilli's legitimate operational and risk-management needs.

Customer remains responsible for the legality of Customer Data submitted to the Services, including notices, permissions, consents, opt-ins, opt-outs, message content, and call-to-action logic initiated through Beacon, Nudge, tilliX, JAREIS, or any other Tilli product.

## 3. Categories of Information We Collect

Depending on the interaction, Tilli may collect the following categories of information:

### 3.1 Information You Provide Directly

- name, email address, phone number, mailing address, employer, title, and business contact details;
- account registration, login, and administrative profile information;
- billing, payment, invoice, and remittance information;
- support requests, chat messages, meeting notes, and communications with Tilli;
- marketing preferences, event registrations, survey responses, and demo requests;
- identity verification, onboarding, or compliance information where relevant to a payment, vendor, or regulated workflow;
- job application materials and recruiting information.

### 3.2 Information Collected Automatically

- IP address, device identifiers, browser type, operating system, and approximate geolocation;
- usage logs, clickstream data, page views, referrers, timestamps, and session activity;
- product telemetry, support diagnostics, crash data, and configuration data;
- cookie, analytics, and similar tracking information, subject to applicable preference controls and legal requirements.

### 3.3 Information Received from Other Sources

- information from customers, partners, or integrations that use the Services;
- identity verification, fraud, sanctions, and compliance-screening providers;
- payment processors, banking, network, or telecom partners;
- advertising, analytics, CRM, marketing, and sales-enablement providers;
- publicly available sources or business-intelligence sources used for enterprise sales, due diligence, or onboarding.

## 4. How We Use Personal Information

Tilli may use personal information to:

- provide, operate, maintain, support, and secure the Site and Services;
- create, manage, authenticate, and administer accounts;
- process transactions, onboarding, implementations, support, and contract administration;
- send operational messages, service notices, security alerts, and product updates;
- power workflow, analytics, orchestration, AI assistance, and event-triggered actions requested by customers;
- respond to inquiries, demos, partnership requests, recruiting inquiries, and support cases;
- improve product performance, usability, reliability, and security;
- prevent fraud, abuse, spam, account misuse, sanctions breaches, money laundering risk, and other unlawful or harmful activity;
- comply with law, enforce contracts, protect rights, and defend claims;
- conduct marketing and sales activities in accordance with applicable law and customer preferences.

## 5. Legal Bases and Processing Grounds

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, Tilli may rely on one or more of the following:

- performance of a contract or taking steps requested before entering into a contract;
- compliance with legal or regulatory obligations;
- Tilli's legitimate interests, including security, fraud prevention, service improvement, enterprise sales, corporate administration, and protection of rights;
- consent, where Tilli or its customers seek and rely on consent for a specific processing activity;
- another basis permitted under applicable law.

## 6. How We Disclose Information

Tilli may disclose personal information to:

- affiliates within the Tilli corporate group;
- service providers, subprocessors, contractors, and advisors that help host, support, secure, analyze, market, or operate the Site or Services;
- payment processors, banks, networks, identity-verification vendors, fraud tools, cloud providers, AI or telecom providers, and other technical providers used to operate the Services;
- customers and authorized users where the disclosure is part of the Services or required to fulfill the customer relationship;
- third-party integrations or counterparties at Customer's direction;
- regulators, law enforcement, courts, government agencies, or counterparties where required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, security incidents, sanctions, AML issues, or contractual abuse;
- parties involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, financing, bankruptcy, or sale of assets.

Tilli does not sell personal information for money. If Tilli engages in cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, or similar activity covered by specific privacy laws, Tilli will address those activities through the applicable notice, consent, or opt-out mechanisms.

## 7. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

Tilli may use cookies, pixels, SDKs, and similar technologies to:

- remember preferences and session information;
- operate core website and product functionality;
- analyze traffic, usage, engagement, and performance;
- support marketing attribution, campaign measurement, and business development;
- improve content, security, and user experience.

Tilli may provide cookie preference tools where required and may honor browser or device-level controls to the extent required by applicable law and technically supported.

## 8. Customer Data and Service Provider Processing

Where Tilli handles personal information on behalf of a customer through the Services, Tilli generally acts as a processor, service provider, or similar vendor and processes that information in accordance with customer instructions, the applicable contract, and Tilli's operational, security, fraud, compliance, and legal obligations.

Customers are responsible for:

- determining whether the Services are appropriate for their use cases;
- providing legally required notices to end users;
- obtaining valid permissions or consent where required;
- configuring retention, workflow, and communications settings appropriately;
- reviewing and approving AI-generated or workflow-generated CTAs before relying on them in regulated or customer-facing contexts where human review is warranted.

## 9. Data Retention

Tilli retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

- to provide the Site and Services;
- to maintain business and contractual records;
- to meet legal, tax, accounting, security, and compliance obligations;
- to investigate fraud, incidents, disputes, or claims;
- to enforce contracts or preserve evidence.

Retention periods may vary by product, data category, customer configuration, jurisdiction, and legal obligation. Tilli may delete, de-identify, aggregate, or anonymize information when it is no longer required to be retained in identifiable form.

## 10. Security

Tilli maintains reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Tilli therefore does not promise that the Site or Services will be immune from every security event, intrusion, or vulnerability. Customers remain responsible for securing their own environments, credentials, endpoints, integrations, and user access controls.

## 11. International Data Handling

Tilli may process and transfer personal information in the United States, India, and other jurisdictions where Tilli, its affiliates, subprocessors, or service providers operate. Where required, Tilli may use contractual, organizational, or technical mechanisms intended to support lawful cross-border processing.

## 12. Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on the applicable law and the person's relationship to Tilli, individuals may have rights to request:

- access to personal information;
- correction of inaccurate information;
- deletion of information;
- restriction of certain processing;
- portability of certain information;
- objection to certain processing;
- withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent;
- opt-out of certain marketing or advertising-related processing.

These rights are not absolute and may be limited by law, security concerns, legal privilege, trade secret protection, fraud prevention, contractual commitments, or Tilli's obligations to customers or regulators.

Marketing emails may generally be opted out of using unsubscribe links or by contacting Tilli. Operational or transactional communications may still be sent where necessary.

## 13. Children's Information

Tilli's Site and Services are not directed to children, and Tilli does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children except where information is provided through a customer-controlled workflow and the customer is responsible for the lawful basis and required notices.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

Tilli may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, operations, products, data practices, or risk posture. When required, Tilli will post the updated policy date and provide additional notice or consent mechanisms.
