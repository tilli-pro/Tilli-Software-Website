# Tilli Consolidated AML, Sanctions, and Financial Crime Compliance Policy

Draft date: May 11, 2026

Status: Draft for internal review, compliance review, and legal review

## 1. Purpose

Tilli is committed to maintaining risk-based policies, procedures, and controls designed to prevent the misuse of its payment, onboarding, disbursement, money-movement, and related Services for money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, terrorist financing, or other unlawful financial activity.

This policy is intended to establish a consolidated framework for anti-money laundering, sanctions, fraud, customer identification, and related financial-crime compliance across Tilli's current and future production products and workflows where those controls are applicable.

## 2. Covered Entities

For United States operations, the relevant entity is **Utilli LLC (doing business as Tilli)**. For India operations, the relevant entity is **Tilli Software India Private Limited**. Those entities are referred to in this policy individually or collectively, as applicable, as **Tilli** or **Tilli Software**.

This policy may be supplemented by processor rules, banking partner requirements, card-network rules, partner onboarding procedures, order forms, or other product-specific compliance controls.

## 3. Scope and Applicability

This policy applies on a risk-based basis to the products, customers, partners, jurisdictions, and workflows for which Tilli reasonably determines AML, sanctions, KYC, KYB, fraud, or financial-crime controls are necessary or appropriate.

It is especially relevant to:

- TilliPay and payment-related workflows;
- digital disbursements, ACH, settlement, or other money-movement services;
- partner, merchant, vendor, or other direct customer onboarding;
- direct customer or partner accounts involving payment or financial functionality;
- workflow-triggered payment or onboarding actions initiated through Beacon, Nudge, tilliX, JAREIS, or related Tilli Services;
- OTP, partner, or referral-party relationships involved in regulated or payments-sensitive services.

This policy does not assume that every SaaS workflow or every end user of a Tilli customer is Tilli's direct regulated customer. Where Tilli provides software tools to a customer that manages its own end-consumer relationship, Tilli may determine that certain AML, KYC, or onboarding obligations primarily remain with the customer or a regulated partner rather than with Tilli directly.

## 4. Governance and Oversight

Tilli will maintain designated responsibility for AML and related financial-crime compliance oversight. Governance responsibilities may include:

- policy ownership and review;
- risk assessment;
- screening and onboarding control design;
- suspicious-activity escalation;
- sanctions and fraud monitoring;
- training coordination;
- testing or audit coordination;
- regulator, partner, or law-enforcement response management where applicable.

Specific role assignments may be maintained in internal compliance records and may be updated without amending this policy.

## 5. Risk-Based Customer and Partner Segmentation

Tilli's compliance controls may vary depending on the relationship and service model. Tilli may distinguish between scenarios such as:

- indirect end users of a customer's SaaS or payment-enabled workflow;
- direct customers such as sub-merchants, merchants, vendors, payees, or business customers onboarded directly by Tilli;
- partners, referral agencies, other third parties, or disbursement counterparties;
- higher-risk customers, counterparties, use cases, geographies, or transaction patterns that require enhanced due diligence.

Tilli may review and classify customers, partners, beneficial owners, and counterparties using a risk-based methodology and may apply more stringent controls to higher-risk categories.

## 6. Customer Identification and Due Diligence

Where applicable, Tilli may require customer, partner, payee, vendor, merchant, or beneficial-owner information before or during onboarding, activation, settlement, or continued use of an applicable Service.

Those controls may include collection and verification of:

- legal name, business name, addresses, and contact details;
- date of birth or date of formation, as applicable;
- tax identification, registration, or business-identification details;
- authorized representative details;
- beneficial ownership information for legal entities;
- purpose of the relationship and expected transaction profile;
- documentary and non-documentary verification information;
- ongoing profile updates and periodic refreshes.

Where required or appropriate, Tilli may conduct customer due diligence, enhanced due diligence, periodic review, and ongoing monitoring. Refusal to provide requested compliance information may result in onboarding denial, delayed activation, restricted access, suspension, rejection of transactions, or termination of the affected relationship or workflow.

## 7. Sanctions and Restricted-Party Screening

Tilli may screen customers, partners, beneficial owners, payees, counterparties, transactions, or related data against sanctions lists, restricted-party lists, geolocation signals, negative news, or other compliance-relevant indicators.

If Tilli identifies a potential match, elevated risk, prohibited geography, or restricted-party concern, Tilli may:

- hold or reject transactions;
- restrict or suspend the affected workflow or account;
- request additional documentation or clarification;
- escalate the matter for manual review;
- notify partners, processors, networks, or authorities where required or appropriate.

## 8. Transaction Monitoring and Suspicious Activity Review

For applicable Services, Tilli may use automated and manual controls to review activity for red flags, anomalies, suspicious behavior, fraud indicators, sanctions concerns, structuring, unusual velocity, account misuse, or other potentially unlawful or unauthorized activity.

Monitoring may consider factors such as:

- transaction amount, frequency, origin, destination, and velocity;
- changes in account behavior or risk profile;
- geographic inconsistency or sanctions exposure;
- unusual or unexplained payment or disbursement activity;
- data mismatches, evasion patterns, or suspicious workflow orchestration;
- attempts to use Beacon, Nudge, tilliX, JAREIS, or other Tilli Services to initiate, mask, automate, or scale prohibited payment or onboarding activity.

Where Tilli determines that activity warrants escalation, Tilli may investigate internally and may file or support the filing of suspicious-activity, currency, funds-transmittal, or other regulatory reports where required or appropriate.

## 9. Holds, Restrictions, and Service Controls

Tilli may delay, reject, reverse where permissible, or otherwise restrict transactions, onboarding steps, activations, settlements, account changes, or automated CTAs when reasonably necessary for:

- fraud prevention;
- sanctions screening;
- KYC, KYB, CIP, CDD, or beneficial-ownership review;
- suspicious-activity investigation;
- legal process response;
- partner, bank, network, or processor requirements;
- safety, security, or reputation protection.

Tilli may also limit functionality, geographies, integrations, transaction types, or customer categories based on product availability, licensing posture, operational readiness, or compliance risk.

## 10. Recordkeeping

Tilli may maintain records relating to onboarding, verification, due diligence, screenings, investigations, compliance decisions, reports, and other AML or sanctions controls for periods reasonably necessary to comply with law, partner requirements, audit expectations, dispute needs, or internal risk-management requirements.

Retention periods may vary by jurisdiction, product, partner, data category, and legal obligation.

## 11. Training

Tilli may provide AML, sanctions, fraud, and financial-crime compliance training to relevant employees, contractors, and operators based on role and risk. Training may address onboarding review, screening, suspicious-activity escalation, data handling, confidentiality, and control operation.

## 12. Testing and Review

Tilli may conduct periodic review, testing, validation, partner review, or independent assessment of applicable AML, sanctions, fraud, and onboarding controls. The scope and frequency may vary by business line, risk level, product maturity, regulatory requirements, and partner expectations.

This policy should be reviewed at least annually and may be updated more frequently to reflect changes in law, risk, product design, banking or processor requirements, and operational experience.

## 13. Confidentiality and Law-Enforcement Cooperation

Compliance investigations, alerts, reports, and related records may be treated as confidential and restricted to those with a legitimate need to know. Tilli may cooperate with regulators, law enforcement, banking partners, processors, or other lawful authorities as required or appropriate.

## 14. Customer and Partner Responsibilities

Customers and partners using applicable Services must:

- provide timely, accurate, and complete compliance information reasonably requested by Tilli;
- avoid using the Services to conceal, structure, facilitate, or automate unlawful financial activity;
- avoid routing activity through Beacon, Nudge, tilliX, JAREIS, or other Tilli tools in a manner intended to evade screening, monitoring, or control thresholds;
- comply with applicable law and with Tilli's product-specific compliance instructions, onboarding requirements, and workflow restrictions.

## 15. Contact and Escalation

Questions regarding this policy, or escalations relating to AML, sanctions, fraud, or suspicious activity, should be directed through Tilli's designated compliance and legal channels maintained in internal governance records.
