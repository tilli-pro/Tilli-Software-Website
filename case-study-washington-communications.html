Transforming the Customer Communication Experience for a Major Utility

Published by Tilli Software • April 19, 2022
Case study in partnership with Washington Gas

Background and Summary

Washington Gas is a regulated utility providing natural gas service to more than 1.2 million customers in the District of Columbia, Maryland, and Virginia. Serving residential, commercial, and industrial customers, Washington Gas sought to modernize its customer communication experience across channels to meet rising expectations and reduce operational costs.

Problem

Customer service advocates grappled with a slow and unwieldy interface for critical tasks such as address changes and phone-based enrollments. Customers often struggled to find actionable information across the website and communication channels, driving higher call center volumes. Washington Gas engaged Tilli to streamline customer communications across email, SMS, push notifications, web, IVR, and mobile—improving customer experience while reducing pressure on service teams and leveraging digital platforms for their 1.2 million customers.

Solution

Tilli’s TilliCX teams delivered an advanced platform that created a consistent, secure communication experience across web and mobile channels. The solution made it faster and safer for customers to complete simple, actionable tasks and enabled the provider to orchestrate personalized, measurable campaigns fully integrated with SAP HANA in real time.

  • Streamlined flows for paperless enrollments, one‑click bill payments, and installment plan enrollments.
  • Digitally signed attestations for Covid Relief Funds, payment tokenization, digital signature, and payment collection for commitment letters.
  • Empowered service advocates to plan, design, and execute actionable, personalized campaigns via an SAP‑integrated online editor.
  • Real‑time integration with SAP backend systems ensured a single source of truth for communications and transactions.

Results

Customers gained better self‑service control through targeted, behavior‑based campaigns and activities. Service teams adapted to streamlined tools and processes, improving efficiency and outcomes.
