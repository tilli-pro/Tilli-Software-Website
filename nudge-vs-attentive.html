The short version: Nudge SMS costs $0.00586 per message vs Attentive's $0.01200 — about 51% cheaper — and skips Attentive's $100/month platform minimum. Attentive is purpose-built for e-commerce marketing SMS; Nudge is purpose-built for billing notifications, payment reminders, and operational alerts. Different jobs, different fits.

Bottom line:

Attentive is excellent if you're a Shopify-style retailer running abandoned-cart and post-purchase SMS. If you're sending billing notifications, payment reminders, outage alerts, or OTP for a regulated business — Nudge is half the per-message cost, has no platform minimum, and ships with the compliance and templates Attentive's e-commerce focus doesn't cover.

Frequently asked: Nudge vs Attentive

Is Nudge cheaper than Attentive?

Yes — substantially. Nudge sends 5,000 SMS for $29.30/month ($0.00586 per message). Attentive charges $60.00/month at the same volume ($0.01200 per message) plus a $100/month platform minimum on top — making Nudge roughly 51% cheaper before you even factor in Attentive's minimum fee.

Does Attentive charge a platform minimum?

Yes. Attentive has a $100/month platform fee on top of per-message charges. That makes the effective entry price ~$160/month for 5,000 SMS. Nudge has no platform minimum — you pay $29.30 for 5,000 SMS, period.

Is Attentive built for e-commerce or for utility-style billing?

Attentive is built primarily for e-commerce SMS marketing — abandoned-cart messages, promotions, drip campaigns. Nudge is built for transactional and notification SMS — billing alerts, payment reminders, outage notifications, OTP — for utilities, healthcare, insurance, and financial services. The two products solve different problems.

Can Nudge replace Attentive for SMS marketing campaigns?

Yes for most campaigns. Nudge supports segmentation, drip flows, A/B testing, and broadcast SMS. Attentive's strength is its e-commerce conversion-optimization tooling (abandoned-cart, browse-abandonment, post-purchase) which is specific to retail. If you're a regulated-industry brand sending billing or service notifications via SMS, Nudge is a better fit.

Does Nudge support 2-way SMS, short codes, and toll-free?

Yes. Nudge supports 2-way messaging, short codes, toll-free SMS numbers, MMS, and conversational AI auto-responses out of the box, at parity with Attentive.

What about email, WhatsApp, and voice — does Attentive offer those?

Attentive launched email and direct mail products in recent years but its core remains SMS marketing. It does not offer WhatsApp Business, voice/IVR, or push notifications. Nudge includes all of those channels in the same plan as SMS.

Is Nudge TCPA-compliant for SMS marketing?

Yes. Nudge ships with TCPA and FDCPA tooling for SMS — opt-in management, quiet-hours enforcement, double-opt-in flows, and STOP/HELP keyword handling. Both Nudge and Attentive support TCPA-compliant patterns; Attentive's marketing-first focus means more retail-style consent flows, while Nudge supports billing and collections-style patterns natively.
