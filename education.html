Tools That Power the Modern Campus

Nudge — Timely, Personalized Campus Communication

Proactively connect with students, parents, and faculty through SMS, Email, WhatsApp, In-App, and Voice channels.

Use Cases

  • Tuition & fee payment reminders
  • Registration & class schedule alerts
  • Financial aid application nudges
  • Transcript request & document submission prompts
  • Campus safety notifications & closures
  • Orientation, event, and deadline announcements

tilliPay — Flexible, Student-Centric Payment Experiences

Enable fast, secure payments across all student financial interactions. Supports multiple payment methods, auto-pay setups, and branded pay-by-link experiences.

Use Cases

  • Tuition, housing, and meal plan payments
  • Application and registration fees
  • Payment plans, scholarships, and refunds
  • Study abroad and program-specific payments
  • Event or merchandise sales

Payment Methods Supported: ACH, Debit/Credit Cards, Digital Wallets, Pay-by-Link

tilliX — Real-Time Insights for Enrollment & Finance Teams

Gain full visibility into student engagement, payment behavior, and communication effectiveness to drive smarter decisions and early interventions.

Use Cases

  • At-risk student flagging based on behavior
  • Financial aid & payment plan engagement analytics
  • Email/SMS campaign effectiveness
  • Dashboard views for bursars, registrars, and student affairs
  • Year-over-year enrollment and payment trends

Views Available: Role-Based Dashboards, Engagement Analytics, Program-Level Insights
