CYBER RESILIENCE USE CASE

Maintain Operations During Cyber Incidents

Transform Nudge into a resilient customer intelligence engine that ensures billing continuity and communication capabilities even when primary systems are compromised.

View Solution Contact Sales

Executive Summary

A dual-purpose investment that delivers operational resilience and marketing intelligence

This enhancement evolves Nudge from a communication delivery platform into a resilient customer intelligence engine that supports both cyber resiliency and advanced customer segmentation.

By integrating CMDR, tilliArc, and tilliX, Nudge gains the ability to ingest weekly emergency data, generate and archive digital bills, and securely deliver them to customers through email or SMS, even in the event of system isolation.

Key Capabilities

Operational Continuity

Maintain customer billing and communication continuity during cyber events with offline-capable repository and delivery systems.

Unified Intelligence Layer

Single data model that merges customer, billing, and account details into a queryable intelligence platform.

Secure Bill Archive

tilliArc provides secure PDF storage, dynamic expiring link generation, and compliance-ready archival retrieval.

Advanced Segmentation

Target customers by rate class, payment patterns, geography, and bill status for precise communications.

Soft Authentication

tilliX integration enables secure bill viewing with ZIP/code verification without requiring full account login.

Multi-Channel Delivery

Send personalized bill notifications with secure links via email, SMS, WhatsApp, and other channels.

System Architecture

Data Flow Architecture

1

Secure File Ingestion

Weekly emergency billing file received via secure FTP from utility systems

2

ETL & Data Mapping

ETL service maps emergency data to CMDR schema (Customer, Account, Invoice tables)

3

PDF Bill Generation

Nudge or tilliArc converts bill data to PDF format using digital templates

4

Archive & Link Creation

PDFs stored securely in tilliArc with dynamic expiring short links generated

5

Customer Notification Delivery

Nudge sends personalized notifications with bill details and secure links via email/SMS

6

Secure Bill Access

Customers access bills through tilliX soft-auth (ZIP/code verification)

Implementation Roadmap

1

Foundation - CMDR Enhancement

Extend CMDR schema to support emergency billing data, enhance data ingestion pipelines, and establish secure FTP connectivity.

Deliverables: Extended database schema, ETL pipelines, secure file transfer setup
2

Bill Generation & Archive

Develop PDF bill generation templates, integrate tilliArc for secure storage, and implement dynamic short link creation.

Deliverables: Bill templates, tilliArc integration, link management system
3

Communication & Delivery

Configure Nudge templates with smart merge tags, set up multi-channel delivery workflows, and implement tilliX soft authentication.

Deliverables: Message templates, delivery workflows, soft-auth integration
4

Segmentation & Intelligence

Build segmentation API, develop campaign builder integration, and create business intelligence dashboards.

Deliverables: Segmentation API, campaign tools, BI dashboards, analytics
