1. Why Upgrade to S/4HANA?

The shift to SAP S/4HANA promises more than just an ERP facelift—it’s a transformative leap toward real-time analytics, streamlined enterprise processes, and cloud-readiness. S/4HANA unlocks predictive capabilities and offers flexible deployment options across finance, procurement, sales, and more.

Organizations running legacy systems such as ECC face diminishing SAP support and increasing pressure to future‑proof operations—making the move to S/4HANA both strategic and time‑sensitive.

2. Public vs. Private Cloud: What to Consider

Choosing between public and private cloud hinges on customization needs, control preferences, and regulatory requirements. Public cloud offers agility, rapid innovation, and simplified management, while private cloud provides deeper configuration options and enhanced privacy controls for complex, highly regulated environments.

3. Common Concerns and FAQs about Migration

  • Will migration disrupt operations? With structured planning and phased cutovers, disruptions are minimized.
  • What challenges lie ahead? Expect work across data migration, compatibility, process redesign, testing, and user training.
  • How long does it take? Timelines vary by scope and system complexity—assessments provide realistic plans.
  • Which cloud route should we choose? Tilli experts help analyze fit for public vs. private cloud based on your goals and constraints.
