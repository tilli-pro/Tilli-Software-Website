Helping 30,000 People Find COVID-19 Relief

Published by Tilli Software • April 19, 2022
Case study in partnership with Washington Gas

Background and Summary

Washington Gas is a regulated utility providing natural gas service to more than 1.2 million customers in the District of Columbia, Maryland, and Virginia. The clean‑burning natural gas supplied by Washington Gas is the fuel of choice for more than nine out of ten new home buyers in the D.C. metro area.

In March 2020, COVID‑19 was officially declared a pandemic. Moratoria on utility shut‑offs across 32 states and D.C. halted collections, deferring outstanding utility bills for 12–24 months and creating revenue gaps for Washington Gas.

Problem

In November 2020, Virginia released COVID relief funds to help customers pay their utility bills. Washington Gas needed to quickly communicate availability and collect digitally signed attestations from eligible customers.

Solution

Washington Gas partnered with Tilli to deploy a rapid, multi‑channel communications approach to:

  • Make customers aware of available relief options
  • Obtain digitally signed attestations as proof for regulatory authorities
  • Help customers receive funds to pay outstanding utility bills

Tilli met customers where they are—using personalized email, customized direct mail, interactive voice response (IVR), ringless drops, and SMS. Phase 1 launched within two weeks of requirements.

Results

After collecting digital attestations, Washington Gas assisted 30,000 people by applying available funds to delinquent accounts—demonstrating the power of multi‑channel outreach and superior service.

Speed to Credit Attestations – Phase 1

Developed and launched in 20 days. In the first phase, Washington Gas sent 60,469 emails to qualified customers:

  • Emails in December 2020 — 24,853
  • Emails in February 2021 — 35,616

Speed to Credit Attestations – Phase 2

Developed and launched in 10 days. Washington Gas introduced IVR/ringless drops and SMS in addition to email. Totals included 88,486 outbound calls, 117,025 texts, and additional emails sent.

Bottom line
UtiliCX platform successfully achieved COVID‑19 Customer Credit Attestations, resulting in over $8M in customer credits via two multi‑channel campaigns within the open attestation periods allowed by the State Corporation Commission.
