Tools That Power the Telecom Experience

tilliX — Experience-Centric Dashboards

Role-based dashboards scale with massive subscriber bases to deliver insights tailored for different teams.

  • For Subscribers: Self-service portal with plan details, profile settings, billing history, and communication preferences
  • For CSRs: Fast access to subscriber status, recharge history, support tickets, and opt-in/out logs
  • For Admins & Product Teams: Analytics on campaign impact, churn indicators, KYC compliance, payment trends, and regional engagement

Use Cases: Subscriber profile management, KYC workflows, multi-account views, churn risk prediction, bundle behavior tracking, activation flows, and more

Views Available: Subscriber Portal View, CSR Support View, Admin Dashboard, Regional & Product Analytics View

Nudge — Timely, Targeted Subscriber Communication

Deliver contextual alerts and reminders that prompt subscriber actions—across multiple channels.

Use Cases: Billing due reminders, low balance prompts, data pack activations, roaming nudges, plan upgrades, KYC expiry notices, overload warnings, emergency alerts, device trade-in offers

Channels: SMS, Email, WhatsApp, IVR, Mobile Push, In-App

tilliPay — Secure, Flexible Payment Journeys

Designed for scalability and flexibility, tilliPay simplifies payments for diverse telecom billing models.

Use Cases: Postpaid billing with smart links, one-click prepaid recharges, bundled upgrades, split-family billing, KYC fees, refunds, chargebacks, wallet credit, auto-recharge setups

Supported Methods: Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Wallets, UPI, ACH, QR Codes, Pay-by-Link
