Tips to Build Incredible Customer Engagement in Today’s Digital Era

Tilli Software • August 04, 2022 • —

In today’s digital era, customer experience can make or break a brand. The key is building engagement that delights customers consistently across every touchpoint.

The phrase “customer experience” covers every interaction a person has with your products, services, and brand. It includes how customers feel about what is offered and the company behind it.

Customer experience is the new battleground. A Qualtrics study with more than 23,000 consumers showed that eight in ten respondents believe experiences need to improve. With more choice and information than ever, customers expect personalized, relevant, and integrated journeys. Miss these elements and they’ll quickly switch to alternatives.

Three Defining Factors of Great CX

Personalization

People are used to tailored ads and expect similar relevance from brands. Companies should personalize interactions using what they already know and past behaviors.

Effortless

Customers prefer fast, simple flows. Remove friction such as repetitive forms and unnecessary steps.

Omni‑channel

Customers move across phones, tablets, and laptops and want to continue where they left off, on their preferred channel.

Unified Communication: The Need of the Hour

Unified communication helps businesses connect with customers wherever they are—phone, email, text, chat, or social—while logging every interaction in one place.

Choose the best channel for the moment, whether responding to an email query or starting a chat for instant help. This tailored approach shows genuine care for each customer.

Drive CX with Actionable Data

Embrace a data‑driven approach. Map online and offline interactions: search terms, time to find information, frequently visited pages, and more. Use these insights to design VIP‑like experiences.

Personalization is more than custom messages—it’s aligning every component of the journey using real‑time data.

Ways to Improve CX with AI and Data Science

  • Create customized experiences based on prior interactions.
  • Use AI‑powered self‑service so customers resolve issues quickly without agent intervention.
  • Send relevant content via push, email, or SMS to keep customers engaged.

Define and Visualize the Experience You Want

Start with a clear customer journey map. Decide what you want customers to think and feel at each step, then work backward to the content and interactions required.

Collect feedback continuously—at events and across social channels—and keep an eye on industry trends. Then walk through the journey as your customer and refine.

Emotions drive behavior. Every interaction evokes a feeling and leaves an impression. An empathetic approach ensures your brand stays relevant.
