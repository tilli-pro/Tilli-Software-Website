Putting the Power in Your Customer’s Hands: The Rise of the Prosumer

Tilli Software • April 20, 2022 • —

It’s time we give customers what they want—autonomy over money, communication, and data. Most people are now prosumers and expect more say in how they pay and interact with companies. A more intimate, personalized experience is no longer optional.

Your customer experience must shift with digital expectations and accessibility. Here’s what that means.

What Is a Prosumer?

Coined by Alvin Toffler, a prosumer blends producer and consumer—people want hands‑on involvement with the products and services they use. They’re both users and creators, expecting tools and interfaces that let them take an active role.

Design CX for the Prosumer

Audit your business. Do customers have self‑service options? Multiple payment choices? A portal to access information easily? We’re moving from call‑based support to independent, on‑demand interactions.

Upgrade Your Payment Systems

Offer preferences: paper or e‑statements, and modern methods like Apple Pay, Google Pay, crypto, and more. With Utili and Tilli, one‑touch payments and multiple options meet customers where they are.

Upgrade Your Communication Platform

Prosumers want to be informed. Provide real‑time updates and simple access to data via dashboards, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and push. Omnichannel, preference‑based communication creates happier customers.

Upgrade How You Collect Feedback

Encourage input with surveys, instant notifications, and dashboard prompts. Engage on social to build community and gather ideas that improve your services.

Get Comfortable With Real‑Time Updates

Instant information is the benchmark. Real‑time usage and account data empower intentional behavior and better experiences.

Show Up Online

Choose one or two social platforms and start posting consistently. Meet customers where they spend time and build relationships.
