Company Description
Tilli provides an integrated enterprise billing, payment, and communications platform that enables organizations to go 100% digital, reduce costs by 40%, and get paid 3X faster. The platform serves utilities, healthcare, insurance, education, and financial services organizations.

Product Portfolio

Tilli offers four core products that work together as an integrated platform:

tilliX

Enterprise Self-Service Portal & Digital Bill Presentment

Description: tilliX is a white-labeled customer experience and self-service portal platform that enables digital bill presentment (eBill), payment acceptance, account management, and customer self-service capabilities.

Key Features:
  • Digital bill presentment and eBill delivery
  • Self-service customer portal (web and mobile)
  • Payment plan management and automation
  • Dispute resolution and inquiry management
  • Account management and profile updates
  • Real-time payment processing
  • Mobile-responsive design
  • White-label customization
  • Multi-language support
  • WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance
Use Cases:

Bill payment portals, customer self-service, payment plan enrollment, account management, dispute handling, paperless billing adoption

Target Users:

Utility customers, healthcare patients, insurance policyholders, students and parents, banking customers

Learn More:

https://www.tillisoftware.com/tillix

Nudge

CPaaS - Communication Platform as a Service

Description: Nudge is an omnichannel communications platform (CPaaS) for outbound customer engagement including billing notifications, payment reminders, outage alerts, and emergency communications via SMS, email, WhatsApp, voice, and push notifications.

Key Features:
  • Multi-channel messaging (SMS, email, WhatsApp, voice, push)
  • AI-powered send-time optimization
  • Two-way messaging and conversational AI
  • Billing and payment notifications
  • Outage and service disruption alerts
  • Emergency communications
  • Payment reminders and collections messaging
  • Personalization and segmentation
  • Message templates and workflows
  • Delivery tracking and analytics
Use Cases:

Billing notifications, payment reminders, outage alerts, emergency communications, appointment reminders, service notifications, collections communications

Results:

40% increase in message engagement, 35% DSO reduction, 3X faster payment collection

Learn More:

https://www.tillisoftware.com/nudge

tilliPay

Payment Processing & Payment Orchestration

Description: tilliPay is a PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing and payment orchestration platform that accepts and processes payments via ACH, credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets.

Key Features:
  • PCI DSS Level 1 compliance (highest security level)
  • ACH/eCheck processing
  • Credit and debit card acceptance
  • Digital wallet support (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • Recurring payment and autopay management
  • Payment plan processing
  • Failed payment recovery
  • Real-time payment posting
  • Payment reconciliation
  • Tokenization and secure storage
Security & Compliance:

PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA compliant, encryption at rest and in transit

Learn More:

https://www.tillisoftware.com/tillipay

tilliArc

Document Archiving & Compliance Management

Description: tilliArc is a document archiving platform designed for long-term storage, compliance management, and rapid retrieval of billing documents, payment records, and customer communications.

Key Features:
  • Secure document storage and archiving
  • Compliance-ready retention policies
  • Fast search and retrieval
  • Audit trail and version control
  • Integration with billing and payment systems
  • Regulatory compliance support
Use Cases:

Bill archiving, payment record storage, compliance documentation, audit support, historical data retrieval
