Products

tilliX - Enterprise Self-Service Portal

White-labeled customer experience portal with digital bill presentment (eBill), payment plans, dispute resolution, and account management. Integrates with SAP, Oracle, Salesforce.

Results: 60% reduction in support calls, 85% digital adoption rate

Nudge - CPaaS Communications Platform

Omnichannel communications (SMS, email, WhatsApp, voice) for billing notifications, outage alerts, emergency communications, and payment reminders with AI-powered timing.

Results: 40% increase in message engagement, 35% DSO reduction

tilliPay - Payment Processing

PCI DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing platform supporting ACH, credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay).

Results: 3X faster payment collection

tilliArc - Document Archiving

Secure document archiving for compliance and rapid retrieval of billing documents and payment records.
