Leading the Way in Payment Security: Our PCI DSS 4.0 Certification

Tilli Software • January 17, 2025 • —

In today’s digital world, safeguarding customer data is critical. We’re proud to announce that we are now certified under PCI DSS 4.0, the latest and most robust version of the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard. As a provider of card data and payment gateway solutions, we fully understand how essential security is for both our clients and their customers.

Since 2005, data breaches have been rising in scale and frequency, compromising billions of consumer records globally. In 2023 alone, the United States saw 3,205 data compromises affecting over 353 million individuals, while the average global breach cost hit a record-high of $4.88 million in 2024. These alarming trends underscore why compliance matters now more than ever.

For many organizations, achieving full PCI DSS compliance remains a challenge. In 2020, only 43.4% of businesses maintained full compliance—even though that represents growth from 27.9% in 2019, still short of the 55.4% peak in 2016. In response, PCI DSS v4.0.1 introduces more flexibility for implementing security controls tailored to diverse environments—an essential evolution for the modern payment landscape.

Key Features of PCI DSS 4.0

  • Enhanced Security Measures: Stronger encryption, multi-factor authentication, and more advanced monitoring.
  • Increased Flexibility: Adaptive approaches to compliance for unique business scenarios.
  • Risk-Based Practices: Encourages security strategies built around specific risk contexts.
  • Stronger Authentication: Reinforced access control and user identity protocols.
  • Continuous Compliance: Shifts from periodic audits to ongoing oversight.

The Role of Our Products in Supporting PCI DSS Compliance

Our integrated solutions—including Nudge, tilliX, and Monay—are built with security at their core. They seamlessly support tokenized transactions, secure payment gateways, and multi-channel communication for alerts and reminders. All in all, they enable robust compliance while delivering a smooth customer experience.

How We Secure Card Data and Payment Processing

  • Robust Encryption: Ensuring card data is encrypted during storage and transmission.
  • Advanced Monitoring: Continuous surveillance to detect unauthorized access.
  • Strict Access Controls: Granting access only to authorized personnel, protected by MFA.
  • Regular Audits & Updates: Ongoing evaluations to meet PCI DSS 4.0 requirements and close any gaps.
  • Secure Processing Solutions: Delivering seamless, safe payment processing for every business scale.

Conclusion

Our PCI DSS 4.0 certification showcases our unwavering commitment to delivering secure, reliable, and future-ready payment solutions. By choosing us, you’re partnering with a company dedicated to safeguarding every digital transaction.
