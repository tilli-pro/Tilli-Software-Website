What is Recurring Bill Payment and How to Choose the Best Payment Gateway for Your Subscription Business?

Tilli Software • March 21, 2024

1. What is Recurring Payment Processing?

Recurring payment processing allows businesses to automatically collect payments from customers on a regular schedule—weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually. This system manages collection and reconciliation end to end, providing a smooth, worry-free experience for both businesses and customers.

2. Businesses Ideal for Adopting Recurring Bill Payment Methods

  • Subscription-based services: streaming platforms, software, broadband
  • Membership organizations: gyms, clubs, therapy clinics
  • Utility providers: electricity, water, gas, internet
  • SaaS businesses: ongoing updates, maintenance, and support
  • Financial institutions: credit card repayments and EMIs
  • E-learning platforms: course subscriptions
  • Web hosting and domain registrars: hosting plans and renewals

3. Steps Involved in Recurring Payment Processing

  1. Customer Enrollment: Via web forms, calls, or in-person.
  2. Payment Authorization: Customers share payment details and authorize periodic charges.
  3. Payment Schedule Setup: Agreement on billing frequency.
  4. Invoicing: Invoices are sent on scheduled dates.
  5. Payment Processing: The payment gateway processes charges automatically.
  6. Payment Confirmation: Customers receive confirmations on successful payments.
  7. Payment Reconciliation: Merchants record and reconcile transactions accurately.
  8. Subscription Management: Customers can modify or cancel plans as needed.

4. How to Choose the Best Payment Processor for Recurring Payments

  • Strong Security: Choose a PCI-compliant gateway to protect sensitive customer data.
  • Versatile Payment Support: Accept cards, digital wallets, direct debit/ACH, and multiple currencies.
  • Reporting & Analytics: Use dashboards and exports to track payments and customer patterns.
  • Reliable Support: Ensure responsive onboarding and issue resolution.
  • Seamless Integrations: Look for APIs and webhooks that connect with accounting, eCommerce, subscription, or CRM systems.
