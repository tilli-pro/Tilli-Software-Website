Nudge

Automate critical outreach and guide policyholders through each stage of their journey—from onboarding to claims to policy updates.

  • Premium due and renewal reminders
  • Beneficiary update prompts
  • Policy document e-delivery
  • Claims status notifications
  • Lapsed policy reinstatement nudges
  • Regulatory or product updates

Channels: SMS, Email, ChatBot, WhatsApp, IVR, Mobile Push

tilliX

More than an analytics dashboard—tilliX is a configurable digital portal for policyholders, CS teams, admins, and agents.

  • Coverage and premium visibility for policyholders
  • Real-time claim and refund tracking
  • Lapsed policy reactivation triggers
  • Behavioral segmentation and communication insights

  • Campaign performance by policy segment
