Bottom line:

If email is the only channel you'll ever need, SendGrid is fine — and its developer ecosystem is larger. If you're sending billing notifications, payment reminders, or outage alerts to consumers, you almost always need email + SMS together. Paying SendGrid for email and Twilio for SMS doubles your vendor surface area when Nudge handles both for less.

Frequently asked: Nudge vs SendGrid

Is Nudge cheaper than SendGrid?

Yes. Nudge sends 20,000 emails for $11.72/month ($0.00059 per email). SendGrid charges $19.00/month for the same volume ($0.00095 per email) — roughly 38% more. Nudge also includes SMS, WhatsApp, voice, and push notifications in the same subscription, while SendGrid is email-only.

Does Nudge support transactional and marketing email?

Yes. Nudge handles both transactional email (receipts, password resets, billing notifications, OTP) and marketing email (campaigns, drip sequences, customer engagement) in one platform. SendGrid splits this into separate plans (SendGrid Email API for transactional and SendGrid Marketing Campaigns for marketing) priced separately.

What email deliverability does Nudge offer?

Nudge offers managed deliverability with dedicated IP options, SPF/DKIM/DMARC support, automatic bounce and unsubscribe handling, and inbox-placement monitoring. Average inbox delivery is over 98% for properly authenticated domains, comparable to SendGrid.

Is SendGrid the same company as Twilio?

Yes — SendGrid was acquired by Twilio in 2019. SendGrid remains a separate product with its own dashboard, pricing, and bill. If you use Twilio for SMS and SendGrid for email, you have two separate Twilio-billed products. Nudge unifies both channels under one bill.

Can Nudge replace SendGrid for transactional email at scale?

Yes. Nudge handles enterprise transactional volume — billing notifications, payment receipts, OTP, and outage alerts — for utilities, healthcare, and insurance customers including Con Edison, Washington Gas, and AAA. The REST API supports the same templating, personalization, and webhook patterns SendGrid users expect.

Does Nudge support email templates and dynamic content?

Yes. Nudge ships with industry-specific email templates for billing notifications, payment reminders, and outage alerts, plus a templating engine with dynamic content, conditional logic, and personalization at parity with SendGrid's Dynamic Templates.

How long does it take to migrate from SendGrid to Nudge?

Most teams migrate transactional email in 1–3 weeks. Nudge provides a SendGrid-compatible REST API surface, template import tooling, and migration support for IP warm-up and DNS authentication.
