Streamlining Payment Processes with Tilli and Salesforce Sales Cloud Integration for WGL Energy

Published by Tilli Software • February 13, 2025

Summary

WGL Energy, a subsidiary of AltaGas, partnered with Tilli to modernize and secure their payment processes by integrating Tilli’s Monay Global Payment Stack with Salesforce Sales Cloud. The integration enabled seamless capturing of card and eCheck details and significantly improved the Order→Invoice→Payment and Order→Payment→Invoice workflows. As a Payment Facilitator, Tilli delivered a flexible, secure solution that exceeded the capabilities of traditional payment providers.

Introduction

Client Overview

WGL Energy is a leading energy solutions provider and a subsidiary of AltaGas. They offer electricity, natural gas, and renewable energy solutions to residential, commercial, and government customers. Facing growing complexities in payment processing, WGL Energy sought a modern solution to simplify operations and improve customer experience.

Challenge

WGL Energy needed a secure, flexible platform that integrates seamlessly with Salesforce Sales Cloud to:

  • Capture and securely store payment details (card and eCheck).
  • Streamline and automate Order→Invoice→Payment and Order→Payment→Invoice processes.
  • Enhance operational flexibility beyond traditional payment providers.

Problem Statement

  • Lack of Integration: Existing solutions did not integrate smoothly with Salesforce Sales Cloud, causing manual interventions.
  • Limited Flexibility: Rigid provider processes hindered scalability and customization.
  • Compliance Risks: Handling sensitive payment data required a robust, standards‑compliant approach.

Solution

WGL Energy implemented Tilli’s Monay Global Payment Stack, delivering:

  • Seamless Integration:
    • Effortless integration with Salesforce Sales Cloud.
    • Real‑time, bi‑directional syncing of payment data for both workflows.
  • Secure Payment Processing:
    • Robust security for capturing and handling card and eCheck details.
    • Full compliance with industry standards, including PCI DSS.
  • Enhanced Flexibility:
    • As a Payment Facilitator, Tilli enabled custom workflows and features beyond traditional providers.
    • Tailored solutions aligned with WGL Energy’s operational needs.

Results

  • Operational Efficiency:
    • Automated workflows reduced manual effort by approximately 60%.
    • Streamlined processes saved time across departments.
  • Improved Accuracy:
    • Real‑time integration eliminated data discrepancies.
    • Payment data synchronized seamlessly with Salesforce Sales Cloud.
  • Enhanced Security and Compliance:
    • Reduced risks associated with handling sensitive payment details.
    • Met all regulatory and security standards.
  • Increased Flexibility:
    • Customized workflows matched WGL Energy’s business model.
    • Scalable solution supports future growth.
