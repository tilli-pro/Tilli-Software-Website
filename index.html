Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing and plans.

Can I change plans anytime? +
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any billing adjustments.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial for the Professional plan, and our Starter plan is free forever with basic features.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers for Enterprise customers.
How does message counting work? +
Each SMS, email, or notification sent counts as one message. Unused messages don't roll over to the next month, but overage is available at $0.01 per message.
Do you offer volume discounts? +
Yes, we offer custom pricing for high-volume customers. Contact our sales team to discuss your specific needs and get a tailored quote.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Absolutely! There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time, and you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period.
