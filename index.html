Tilli Software - Enterprise Billing and Payment Platform

Our Products

tilliX - Enterprise Experience & Self-Service Portal

tilliX is our comprehensive self-service portal platform that enables digital bill presentment, payment acceptance, and customer account management. Key features include:

  • Digital bill presentment and eBill enrollment
  • Self-service payment plan management
  • Dispute resolution and case management
  • Account information updates
  • Payment history and transaction tracking
  • Mobile-responsive white-label interface
  • WCAG accessibility compliance
  • Multi-language support

Nudge - Communication Platform as a Service (CPaaS)

Nudge is our CPaaS solution for automated, personalized outbound communications. It supports multiple channels and features:

  • SMS/text messaging for billing notifications
  • Email campaigns for statements and reminders
  • WhatsApp Business API integration
  • Voice/IVR automated calling
  • A/B testing for message optimization
  • Customer segmentation and targeting
  • Real-time event triggers
  • Template library with 100+ use cases
  • TCPA and CAN-SPAM compliance

tilliPay - Payment Processing Platform

tilliPay is our enterprise payment processing platform supporting multiple payment methods:

  • ACH/eCheck processing
  • Credit and debit card payments
  • Digital wallet support (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • Alternative payment methods
  • PCI DSS Level 1 compliance
  • Real-time fraud detection
  • Payment tokenization
  • Recurring payment management
  • Payment plan automation
  • Instant payment verification
