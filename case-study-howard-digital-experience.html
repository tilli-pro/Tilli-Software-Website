Digital Customer Experience for Howard County, Maryland

Published by Tilli Software • April 19, 2022
Case study in partnership with Howard County

Background

Howard County has been one of the fastest‑growing counties since 2017. To better engage constituents, the County sought a fully customizable, agile platform that would drive digital adoption and enable dynamic, behavior‑changing communications.

Problem

When implementing SAP, the County chose not to adopt the recommended online self‑service platform. Concerns included the cost and effort of installing, configuring, hosting, maintaining, and securing an online presence while complying with PCI DSS. Customizing SAP’s solution to align with a premise‑based master data model was cost‑prohibitive. The County engaged Tilli to deliver a branded customer engagement platform providing personalized outreach and communication without the operational burden.

Solution

Tilli delivered a branded web, mobile, email, and SMS solution deployed in the cloud, fully integrated with SAP in real time with a 99.9% uptime SLA. The solution was customized to align with Howard County’s premise‑based SAP implementation and legal requirements.

Results

Deployed on the TilliCX platform with Level 1 PCI DSS compliance. The County earned a NACo Award (2019), and the State of Maryland recognized Tilli for its contribution.

14% Reduction in call center volume
19% Reduction in unpaid bills
50% Increase in digital adoption
