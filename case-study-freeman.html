Freeman Enhances Secure Checkout with tilliX’s Low‑Code BRF Solution

Published by Tilli Software • February 14, 2025

Challenge

Freeman, a leading provider of event management and experiences, faced inefficiencies in their online portal’s checkout process. Their existing system struggled with:

  • Delayed processing of orders, invoices, and payments.
  • A fragmented approach to capturing user consent for transactional processes.
  • Limited agility in deploying and maintaining changes due to a reliance on complex and rigid development cycles.

These challenges impacted operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and compliance, creating the need for a modern, adaptable solution.

Solution: tilliX Low‑Code BRF Implementation

Freeman deployed tilliX’s Business Rules Framework (BRF), a low‑code solution specifically designed for quicker and more flexible deployment and ongoing system maintenance. The BRF empowered Freeman to:

1) Streamline Order‑to‑Invoice and Payment Processes

  • Enabled real‑time, automated processing of orders, invoices, and payments, reducing the manual workload and operational delays.

2) Capture User Consent with Ease

  • Integrated consent management seamlessly into the checkout process. Consent for the Order → Payment → Invoice workflow was securely captured as part of each transaction, ensuring compliance with regulatory standards.

3) Leverage Low‑Code Technology

  • The low‑code architecture allowed rapid system customizations to meet evolving business needs without requiring extensive development efforts.
  • A flexible drag‑and‑drop interface for defining and modifying business rules significantly reduced time‑to‑market for feature updates.

4) Ensure Scalability and Security

  • tilliX’s architecture was built to scale, supporting high transaction volumes with robust security to protect sensitive payment and order data.

Results

1) Enhanced Customer Experience

  • Customers enjoyed a seamless, user‑friendly checkout experience with immediate payment processing and transparent transaction confirmations.
  • The intuitive portal provided clarity and ease of use, improving overall satisfaction.

2) Operational Efficiency

  • Manual intervention was reduced by over 60%, allowing Freeman’s staff to focus on strategic tasks.
  • Automated workflows minimized errors and processing delays.

3) Flexibility and Agility

  • The low‑code BRF enabled Freeman to quickly implement changes, such as adapting to new business rules or regulatory updates, without relying on lengthy development cycles.
  • The framework’s reusability further accelerated the deployment of future enhancements.

4) Compliance and Security

  • Integrated consent management and secure data handling ensured adherence to regulatory standards while fostering trust with users.

5) Scalability

  • The system was designed to support high transactional volumes, making it future‑ready for Freeman’s growing business.
