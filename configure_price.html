Recommended conversation arc

  • 1
    Educate with TCO

    Show the total cost difference vs. Twilio/SendGrid/Mailchimp. Highlight included payment communications and compliance coverage.

  • 2
    Configure tokens

    Let the customer drive the inputs. Capture their preferred channels, payment volumes, and integration needs.

  • 3
    Recommend a tier

    Explain why a given package fits (e.g., “Your 52K tokens map to Professional, giving ~$0.010/token and 50K included”).

  • 4
    Lock the offer

    Summarize savings, add first-month-free reminder, and send them straight into signup.html with plan data pre-loaded.
