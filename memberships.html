Tools That Power the Membership Journey

tilliX: Insightful Dashboards for Membership Teams

Empowers admins, finance leads, and community managers with real-time visibility into member behavior, payment activity, and engagement metrics. Drive outreach and reduce churn with precision.

Use Cases

  • Self-service profile and preference management
  • Event registration and attendance tracking
  • Membership tier management and benefit access
  • Support ticket submission and resolution
  • Payment plan visibility and billing history
  • Engagement dashboards by program, tier, or location
  • Automated alerts for inactive or high-risk members

Views: Member View, Admin View, Finance View, Program Manager View

Nudge: Personalized Communication for Every Member

Delivers contextual, timely messages via email, SMS, WhatsApp, and more—without manual follow-ups.

Use Cases

  • Membership renewal reminders
  • Welcome messages with onboarding checklists
  • Event invitations and RSVP confirmations
  • Tier upgrade prompts and benefit nudges
  • Payment failure notifications and re-engagement flows
  • Birthday and anniversary greetings

Channels: Email, SMS, WhatsApp, In-App, Voice, ChatBot

tilliPay: Flexible Payment Experiences for Member-Based Models

Simplifies dues collection and membership billing across any device or model.

Use Cases

  • Monthly or annual membership dues
  • Family or group plan billing
  • Donation and sponsorship payments
  • Auto-renewal enrollment and management
  • Refunds, chargebacks, discounts, and partial payments
  • Multi-location or multi-program fee separation

Payment Methods Supported: Auto Debit, Credit/Debit Cards, ACH, Wallets, QR Codes, Pay-by-Link

Explore tilliPay

Key Capabilities

  • Send welcome messages with CTAs guiding profile setup, benefits access, and preference selections
  • Promote events or onboarding sessions based on member type or location
  • Deliver tier-specific nudges, family add-on offers, or exclusive benefits
  • Enable one-click upsells via embedded payment links
  • Detect at-risk members using behavioral and payment insights
  • Customize experiences dynamically by age, interests, location, or tier
  • Track engagement trends and payment habits for smarter retention strategies
