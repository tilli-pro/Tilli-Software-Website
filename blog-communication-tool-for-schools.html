The popularity of communication platforms as a service (CPaaS) software has exploded in various verticals, finding its firm footing within the education world. It’s no secret that our education systems are in dire need of a facelift. With the advent of new technologies and communication tools, there is now a chance for us to create something unique for the next generation of students.

The proliferation of the internet, social media, and global connectivity has created an entirely new way of learning and sharing knowledge that can help us change the way our students learn forever when we empower them by adding communication tools for schools.

While using cloud-based CPaaS software solutions is just the tip of the iceberg, they represent a paradigm shift in the world of communication tools. Adopting these software solutions allows teachers to create interactive lessons with their students, administrators to communicate with parents, gives students and faculty a safe way to communicate, boosts overall school security, and more.

Integrated Communication Tool for Schools

Integrated communication is a key component of any successful education institution. This can include student or parent communication, student engagement, and student retention. One of the essential features of a communication tool for faculty, students, and alumni is that it can be integrated into all aspects of your institution’s operations. CPaaS systems allow you to connect with your users across multiple platforms, from email and text messaging to video chat and IVR. Through these solutions, universities are able to connect with faculty, administrative staff, students, and parents regarding upcoming events, class schedules, updates about fee payments, and so on.

When looking at solutions, keep in mind the power of cloud-based tools. The platform should integrate with your existing ERP and SAP services so it’s easy for your staff and faculty members who don’t have experience using CPaaS tools before get started without having too much trouble setting up everything from scratch.

Communication Tool for Schools Helps With Security and Safety

Campus security is a growing concern at many colleges and universities.

CPaaS software solutions can be combined with traditional data like IoT sensors to foster better campus security. For example, a student can press the emergency button, and nearby security guards receive an SMS instantly. The SMS includes location information so that they can immediately go to where the student is located without searching for them in a crowd of people.

Incident reporting is one of the most important benefits of using a communication tool, making it easier and more accurate. If you rely on paper-based forms and emails to report incidents, you risk having incomplete or inaccurate reports, loss of data, or bad information. With a system like this, all information is entered into one database where several different staff members can review it with no confusion about who needs to see what documentation.

Because everyone has access to the same information at the same time (including students), no one has to worry about missing out on crucial details or forgetting something important when they submit their report. It improves transparency and accountability, two vital components of campus safety.

CPaaS software also ensures students are notified of an emergency on campus — whether they’re at home studying in their dorm room or miles away from school visiting home. This feature allows universities across the country not only preventative measures but also quicker responses during times when people need information quickly.

Improve Faculty’s Focus on Teaching

When faculty can focus on teaching, they are more productive, effective, and efficient. This is true for both teachers who teach in person or online. Using a communication tool allows faculty to communicate with all of the students in their class or course at once. They can send announcements about upcoming events or important information. They can communicate with parents who need help or answer questions from parents when they have them.

Using education-specific communication software encourages students to connect with each other’s parents and their professors through an easy-to-use interface that looks familiar and feels comfortable for everyone involved in the process.

Reduce Time-Consuming Administrative Work

  • Helping to streamline communication between teachers and students.
  • Reducing the amount of time spent on data entry during the registration process.
  • Eliminating the need for phone calls or emails between parents and school administrators.

The result? Less administrative work means more time for educators to do what they love.
