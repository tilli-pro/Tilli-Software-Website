Too Many Payment Gateways? Here’s How to Choose the Best One!

Tilli Software • May 4, 2023

Businesses today rely on innovative solutions to engage customers, enhance their experience, optimize operations, and operate efficiently in a secure digital environment. Among these solutions, a payment gateway has become essential for gaining customer trust and confidence for all online transactions. With the rise of digital commerce and UPI in India, businesses—online or offline—cannot afford to operate without a robust payment gateway. Users expect ease, accessibility, and security; businesses need timely payments. A reliable gateway ensures smooth, seamless payments and stronger cash flow.

With numerous providers in the market, selecting the right payment gateway can feel overwhelming. How do you choose the best provider—and why does it matter?

Why Choosing the Right Payment Gateway Matters

Your gateway shapes the checkout experience and protects every transaction. Choosing well improves customer satisfaction, reduces friction, and supports your operating efficiency.

Steps to Choose the Best Payment Gateway

  • How easy is integration with your systems and platforms?
  • What are the costs and service terms?
  • Does it provide robust fraud controls and security?
  • Is the provider compliant with AML and PCI‑DSS policies?
  • How is customer data protected (e.g., tokenization)?
  • Do you require international payments?
  • Do you need EMIs or Buy Now, Pay Later options?
  • What additional benefits and insights are available?
  • Does it support UPI, RuPay, cards, netbanking, wallets, ACH, gift cards, and more?

Factors to Consider

Compatibility and Ease of Integration

Ensure compatibility with your website, cart, or eCommerce platform. Prefer gateways with clear APIs and plugins so integration is smooth and UX remains uninterrupted.

Safety and Security

Look for multi‑factor authentication, encryption, tokenization, and PCI‑DSS certification to safeguard sensitive data.

Pricing and Service Agreements

Assess fees based on transaction types, volumes, and frequency. Check for setup or monthly fees and evaluate total cost of ownership.

Added Features and Functionality

Global payment support, electronic invoicing, multiple payment types, reminders, chargeback management, and analytics can be critical differentiators.

Customer Support

Reliable support via phone, email, or chat ensures payment issues are resolved quickly.
