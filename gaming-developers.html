Tools That Power the Player Experience

Nudge — In-App Messaging That Drives Action

Deliver messages to players at the perfect moment—inside the app and in real time. Whether launching a flash sale, guiding a reward, or promoting an upsell, Nudge ensures communications feel personal and timely.

Use Cases

  • In-app push alerts for missions, rewards, and flash sales
  • Event reminders and match-start notifications
  • Behavior-based reward unlock and upsell nudges
  • Cross-platform campaigns across mobile, web, and console
  • Win-back flows for churned or inactive players
  • Offers tailored by spend patterns or in-game progress

Channels: In-App Push, App Notifications, Email, SMS, WhatsApp

tilliPay — Seamless Payment Flows Built for Players

Supports frictionless in-app and multi-platform transactions—whether one-time purchases or battle pass renewals.

Use Cases

  • In-app purchases: power-ups, skins, avatars
  • Battle pass and timed subscription passes
  • Flash or seasonal bundles with expirable checkout links
  • Multi-currency support for global players
  • Refund handling and auto-renewal setup

Payment Methods Supported: Credit/Debit Cards, Wallets, QR Codes, Pay-by-Link

tilliX — Insights & Monetization Analytics

Track audience behavior and monetization funnels in real time to identify drop-off points, churn signals, and opportunities to optimize offers and timing.

Use Cases

  • Purchase funnel analytics and A/B testing
  • Churn risk and win-back opportunity detection
  • Engagement metrics by cohort, event, and channel
  • Revenue impact by offers, bundles, and schedules
