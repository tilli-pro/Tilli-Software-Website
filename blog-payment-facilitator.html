What Are Payment Facilitators and How They Simplify and Accelerate Your Payment Processes

Tilli Software • June 26, 2023

1. Understanding Payment Facilitators (PayFacs)

A Payment Facilitator (PayFac) acts as an intermediary between merchants and payment processors, offering a streamlined way for sub‑merchants to accept payments. Unlike traditional models, merchants don’t need to obtain separate merchant accounts—PayFacs handle the infrastructure, onboarding, and compliance, making it faster and easier to get started.

2. Key Features of the PayFac Model

  • Unified Payments Platform – Simplifies processing across merchants.
  • Sub‑Merchant Onboarding – Rapid, low‑effort enrollment without individual merchant accounts.
  • Risk & Compliance Management – Underwriting, fraud protection, and PCI DSS implementation.
  • Aggregated Processing & Reporting – Consolidated payments and unified dashboards for sub‑merchants.
  • Scalability – Easily scales across merchants without duplicating infrastructure.

3. Benefits for Merchants Using a PayFac

  • Faster Onboarding – Start processing transactions in minutes, not weeks.
  • Reduced Complexity – Compliance, underwriting, and fraud monitoring handled for you.
  • Lower Setup Costs – No individual merchant accounts or custom infrastructure required.
  • Improved Customer Experience – Unified, seamless payment flows build trust.
  • Flexible Growth – Infrastructure adapts as your business scales.

4. Choosing the Right Payment Facilitator

  • Industry Experience – Domain expertise matters.
  • Seamless Integration – Ensure compatibility with existing systems.
  • Supported Payment Methods – Versatility across cards, wallets, and more.
  • Security & Compliance – Verify PCI DSS and regulatory posture.
  • Transparent Pricing – Understand fee structures and total cost of ownership.
  • Quality of Support – Reliable, responsive service is critical.
  • Scalability & Flexibility – The PayFac should grow with you.
