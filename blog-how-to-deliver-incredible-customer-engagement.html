How to Deliver an Incredible Customer Engagement Experience

Tilli Software • June 01, 2022 • —

We live in a digital world, where customer experience is king. So the question is, how do you create a customer engagement experience that feels like magic?

The customer experience is a broad concept that includes your interactions with a company and its products or services. It’s not just about the product or service itself, but how the customers feel about those products and services — and the company offering them.

Customer experience is the new battleground for companies. One Qualtrics study of more than 23,000 consumers showed that 8/10 of respondents believe customer experiences need to be improved. In the digital age, your customers are empowered to make decisions, and they have access to more information than ever before. You need to meet them where they are with an experience that is personalized, relevant and integrated into their lives.

Customers expect their experience to be defined by three things

Personalization

We’re all used to seeing ads tailored to our interests and preferences — and we expect the same from companies themselves. Companies need to tailor interactions based on what they know about us and our past behavior.

Effortless

Customers don’t want to jump through hoops or fill out repetitive forms when trying to make purchases or find information — they want everything with the least possible effort.

Omnichannel

People expect their devices — phones, tablets, laptops — to work seamlessly together so they can use whichever device is most convenient without losing context from previous interactions.

Unified Customer Experience

Unified communication has become popular as businesses realize they must interact with customers regardless of location or device. Set up multiple channels — phone, email, text, chat, WhatsApp, IVR — and route them through one system so everything is logged and organized.

Choose the best method for the moment, whether an email response or a chat on your website. This personalized approach shows customers they’re not just another transaction; they’re people to be listened to and cared for.

Data‑Driven Customer Experience

Think of customer experience as something you build with your data. With the right approach, you can design VIP‑level experiences.

Track how customers interact with your brand online and offline: what they search, how long they take to find things, which pages they visit and for how long, and more. Understanding behavior reveals what customers want and how they want it delivered, so you can tailor experiences specifically to them.

This extends beyond personalized messages; it’s about an entire system running smoothly on real‑time information.

Use AI and Data Science to Improve CX

  • Create personalized experiences based on previous interactions.
  • Build self‑service systems so customers solve problems without contacting support.
  • Deliver relevant content via push notifications, emails, or SMS to keep customers returning.

Create a Vision of Your Customer’s Experience

When building your digital presence, begin with a clear vision or journey map. Decide how you want users to feel and think at each step and work backward to the content and interactions required. Gather feedback from customers through events and social channels, and study industry trends to learn what works.

Role‑play as the customer and refine the journey. Humans are driven by emotion; every interaction leaves an imprint. Lead with empathy to keep your brand relevant.
